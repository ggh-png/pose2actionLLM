The only joints are left/right shoulder, elbow, hip, knee. Blue is anatomical left; orange is anatomical right. There are no wrists, ankles, or chair. Joint labels identify the dots. The plot uses x right and y down; canvas margins are not coordinates.

Decide POSTURE and ARM POSITION separately.

POSTURE: Look at the shoulder-hip-knee shape, especially the thigh segments from hips to knees.
- SITTING: the torso meets sideways thighs at a clear corner at the hips. Knees extend sideways from hips, often near hip height. A chair is NOT required.
- STANDING: the torso and thighs form a mostly extended vertical structure. Knees lie well below the hips, with little sideways displacement. Shoulders, hips, and knees are approximately aligned.
Do not use raised arms to decide sitting versus standing. Both sitting and standing can have either arm position.

ARM POSITION: Look only at each shoulder and its corresponding elbow.
- HAND_UP: at least one elbow is above, level with, or very close to the height of its shoulder. One raised side is enough.
- HAND_DOWN: both available elbows are clearly below their shoulders.
The elbow is a proxy for arm raising; do not invent wrists or hands.
